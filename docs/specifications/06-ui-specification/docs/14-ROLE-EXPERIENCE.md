# 14 --- Role Experience

-   **Cashier** --- Home: `UI-POS-001`. Fast sale/return/collection
    work; no unrestricted administration.
-   **Storekeeper** --- Home: `UI-INV-001`. Receiving, stock visibility
    and counts.
-   **Purchase** --- Home: `UI-PUR-001`. Supplier, PO, GRN and
    purchase-return workflows.
-   **Accounts** --- Home: `UI-ACC-001`. Credit, payables, financial
    accounts, expenses and reconciliation.
-   **Manager** --- Home: `UI-SYS-003`. Store-wide operations,
    approvals, exceptions and reports.
-   **Admin** --- Home: `UI-SYS-003`. Configuration, users,
    stores/counters, devices and support.
-   **Management** --- Home: `UI-SYS-003`. Read-oriented KPIs/reports
    with permissions.
-   **Audit** --- Home: `UI-AUD-001`. Read-only audit/ledger
    investigation.
-   **Support** --- Home: `UI-SUPT-001`. Time-limited
    diagnostics/support only.
